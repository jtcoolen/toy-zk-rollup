\section{Shielded state: keys, notes, trees}
\label{sec:shielded-state}

This section fixes the objects that make up the shielded state: the spend key
pair, the note and its two derived digests (commitment and nullifier), the
append-only note-commitment tree, and the sparse nullifier tree. For each
object it gives the native definition (the \code{shielded} crate, which
describes itself as the executable specification of the rules the STARK
proves \src{crates/shielded/src/lib.rs:6-9}) and, where the circuit
re-derives the object, the in-circuit relation actually enforced. Notation
follows Section~\ref{sec:primitives}: $\Hp(\cdot)$ is the Poseidon2 byte hash
over little-endian 16-bit limbs, $\Cp$ is the Poseidon2 two-to-one
compression, $\|$ is byte concatenation, and a digest is $8$ canonical
$\F$-elements $d_0,\dots,d_7$ serialized as $\mathrm{LE32}(d_0)\|\cdots\|\mathrm{LE32}(d_7)$
\src{crates/pq-hash/src/poseidon.rs:155-163}.

\subsection{Hash instantiation in the shielded layer}
\label{subsec:ss-hashes}

The \code{shielded} crate is generic over two unrelated traits,
\code{ShieldedHasher} (spend key, nullifier) and \code{CommitmentHasher}
(note commitment, tree nodes) \src{crates/pq-hash/src/traits.rs:19-44}. Every
production path instantiates both with Poseidon2:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l l X@{}}
\toprule
Object & Trait & Instance & Where instantiated \\
\midrule
$\mathsf{pk}_d$ & \code{ShieldedHasher} & \code{Poseidon2Shielded}
  & \src{crates/wallet-wasm/src/lib.rs:347} \src{crates/prover/src/fixtures.rs:108} \\
$\mathsf{nf}$ & \code{ShieldedHasher} & \code{Poseidon2Shielded}
  & \src{crates/prover/src/client.rs:204} \src{crates/wallet-wasm/src/lib.rs:536} \\
$\mathsf{cm}$ & \code{CommitmentHasher} & \code{Poseidon2Commitment}
  & \src{crates/prover/src/client.rs:147,155} \\
tree node & \code{hash\_pair} & \code{Poseidon2Commitment}
  & \src{crates/prover/src/client.rs:122} \\
SMT node & \code{hash\_pair} & \code{Poseidon2Commitment}
  & \src{crates/prover/src/client.rs:123,199} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\code{Poseidon2Commitment} implements \code{hash} as the byte hash $\Hp$ and
overrides \code{hash\_pair} with $\Cp$
\src{crates/pq-hash/src/poseidon.rs:225-243}. \code{Poseidon2Shielded} prepends
the domain tag as an extra part and calls the same \code{Poseidon2Commitment::hash}
\src{crates/pq-hash/src/shielded.rs:96-105}. Consequently
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:shielded-hash}
  \code{Poseidon2Shielded.hash\_to\_digest}(\mathit{dom}, (p_1,\dots,p_k))
  \;=\; \Hp(\mathit{dom}\,\|\,p_1\,\|\cdots\|\,p_k),
\end{equation}
with no length prefixes \src{crates/pq-hash/src/shielded.rs:87-94}: the
spend-key, nullifier and commitment digests are all the \emph{same} function
$\Hp$ applied to different byte strings, and their separation rests on the
distinct domain tags together with the fixed preimage lengths of
Table~\ref{tab:ss-preimages}.

\begin{specdelta}[Stale hash names in comments]
\label{delta:ss-sha3}
Several comments still name SHA3-256 or Keccak-256 for these objects: the
spend-key derivation (``SHA3-256 preimage knowledge''
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:36-38}), the commitment (``Keccak-256''
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:104-106}), the nullifier (``SHA3-256''
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:118-120}), the trait contracts
\src{crates/pq-hash/src/traits.rs:10-12,24-32}, and the nullifier tree (``keeps
Keccak'') \src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:30-31}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:15-16}
\src{crates/pq-hash/src/poseidon.rs:19-22}. The enforced instantiation is
Poseidon2 throughout, as tabulated above and as the circuit itself states
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:30-43}
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:12-18}. The SHA3/Keccak instances
appear only in unit tests of the \code{shielded} crate
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:185}
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:364}. See \finding{I-02}.
\end{specdelta}

\subsection{Keys}
\label{subsec:ss-keys}

\begin{definition}[Spend secret]
The spend secret is a byte string $\mathsf{sk}_d \in \{0,1\}^{256}$. The
\code{shielded} crate has no type for it and only receives it as a borrowed
\code{[u8; 32]} \src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:11-16}. The wallet draws it as
$32$ bytes from an RNG seeded by caller entropy
\src{crates/wallet-wasm/src/lib.rs:300-303}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Spend public key]
\label{def:ss-pk}
With $\code{DOMAIN\_PK} = \code{"pq-rollup/spendpk/v1"}$ ($20$ bytes)
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:29},
\begin{equation}
  \mathsf{pk}_d \;=\; \Hp\big(\code{DOMAIN\_PK}\,\|\,\mathsf{sk}_d\big) \in \{0,1\}^{256}.
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:40-42}
The preimage is $52$ bytes, i.e.\ $26$ limbs, absorbed in rate-$8$ chunks of
sizes $8,8,8,2$ (a short final chunk; cf.\ Spec delta~\ref{delta:overwrite}).
\code{SpendPublicKey} is an opaque 32-byte newtype
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:49-64}.
\end{definition}

The tag length is even by design so that the preimage is limb-aligned
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:22-28}; the byte hash asserts an even total
length \src{crates/pq-hash/src/poseidon.rs:231-235}.

\begin{specdelta}[$\mathsf{pk}_d$ is not a signature verifying key]
The module documentation describes \code{SpendPublicKey} as ``the SPHINCS+
verifying key, published in the note preimage''
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:5-7}. The enforced $\mathsf{pk}_d$ is the
Poseidon2 digest of Definition~\ref{def:ss-pk}; the wallet generates the
SPHINCS+ key pair independently of $\mathsf{sk}_d$ from the same RNG stream
\src{crates/wallet-wasm/src/lib.rs:300-305} and stores both secrets side by side
\src{crates/wallet-wasm/src/lib.rs:309-312}. No relation between $\mathsf{pk}_d$
and the SPHINCS+ verifying key is computed; see \finding{M-02}.
\end{specdelta}

\begin{definition}[Incoming viewing key]
\code{IncomingViewingKey} is an opaque 32-byte newtype with a constructor and
an accessor only \src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:80-95}.
\end{definition}

\begin{specdelta}[No viewing-key derivation]
The documentation states $\mathsf{ivk} = H(\code{DOMAIN\_IVK}\,\|\,\mathsf{sk}_d)$
\src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:77-79}, but no \code{DOMAIN\_IVK} constant and
no derivation function exist in the workspace; the type is only constructed
from raw bytes \src{crates/shielded/src/keys.rs:85-87}.
\end{specdelta}

\subsection{Notes, commitments and nullifiers}
\label{subsec:ss-notes}

\begin{definition}[Note]
A note is a tuple
\[
  n = (v, \rho, \psi, \mathsf{pk}_d), \qquad v \in [0, 2^{64}),\ \ \rho,\psi \in \{0,1\}^{256},\ \ \mathsf{pk}_d \in \{0,1\}^{256},
\]
stored as \code{u64}, \code{[u8;32]}, \code{[u8;32]}, \code{SpendPublicKey}
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:48-58}. \code{Note::new} performs no validation
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:66-74}.
\end{definition}

The value domain is restricted elsewhere: natively, \code{check\_balance}
rejects any input, output or fee value above
$\code{MAX\_VALUE} = 2^{62}-1$ \src{crates/shielded/src/transfer.rs:45,77-84};
in-circuit, each amount is four private 16-bit limbs with the top limb
range-checked to $14$ bits, so $v < 2^{62}$
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:111-125,157-172}.

\begin{definition}[Note commitment]
\label{def:ss-cm}
With $\code{DOMAIN\_NOTE} = \code{"pq-rollup/note-commit/v1"}$ ($24$ bytes)
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:38},
\begin{equation}
  \mathsf{cm}(n) \;=\; \Hp\big(\code{DOMAIN\_NOTE}\,\|\,\mathrm{LE64}(v)\,\|\,\rho\,\|\,\psi\,\|\,\mathsf{pk}_d\big).
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:108-112}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Nullifier]
\label{def:ss-nf}
With $\code{DOMAIN\_NULLIFIER} = \code{"pq-rollup/nullifier/v1"}$ ($22$ bytes)
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:41},
\begin{equation}
  \mathsf{nf}(n, \mathsf{sk}_d) \;=\; \Hp\big(\code{DOMAIN\_NULLIFIER}\,\|\,\mathsf{sk}_d\,\|\,\rho\big).
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:125-127}
\end{definition}

\begin{property}[What the nullifier depends on]
\label{prop:ss-nf-inputs}
$\mathsf{nf}$ is a function of $(\mathsf{sk}_d, \rho)$ only: it does not
depend on $v$, $\psi$, $\mathsf{pk}_d$ or the note's position in the
commitment tree \src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:125-127}. Two notes with the
same $(\mathsf{sk}_d,\rho)$ therefore share a nullifier; for output notes
$\rho$ is constrained in-circuit only by its 16-bit limb range checks
(Table~\ref{tab:ss-circuit-notes}). See \finding{M-08}.
\end{property}

\paragraph{In-circuit re-derivation.} The transfer circuit recomputes all three
digests with the in-circuit sponge \code{p2\_sponge\_limbs}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:306-367}, prepending the domain tag

byte string is allocated as 16-bit limbs, each range-checked to $16$ bits

is \emph{derived} from $\mathsf{sk}_d$ and fed into the commitment preimage as
its exported limbs $L(\mathsf{pk}_d)$ (Section~\ref{subsec:digest-enc})

is a circuit constant \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:573-580}.

\subsection{Note-commitment tree}
\label{subsec:ss-cmtree}

\begin{definition}[Shape and empty subtrees]
\label{def:ss-empties}
The commitment tree is a binary Merkle tree of fixed depth
$D = \code{DEPTH} = 32$ (capacity $2^{32}$ leaves)
\src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:39}. A leaf is the 32-byte commitment
$\mathsf{cm}$ viewed as a digest \src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:46-48}.
The empty-subtree digests are
\begin{equation}
  E_0 = 0^{256} \ (\text{all-zero digest}), \qquad E_h = \Cp(E_{h-1}, E_{h-1}) \quad (1 \le h \le D),
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:65-73}
and the empty-tree root is $E_D$ \src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:85-87}. The
empty leaf $E_0$ is the same byte string as an all-zero leaf digest.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Root]
For leaves $\ell_0,\dots,\ell_{n-1}$ appended in order ($n \le 2^D$), define for a
leaf range $[s,e)$ at height $h$
\begin{equation}
  N(s,e,h) \;=\;
  \begin{cases}
    E_h & s \ge e,\\
    \ell_s & h = 0,\\
    \Cp\big(N(s,m,h-1),\,N(m,e,h-1)\big), \ \ m = \min(s+2^{h-1}, e) & \text{otherwise},
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:223-235}
and $\mathsf{root} = N(0, n, D)$ \src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:214-216}.
\code{append} assigns index $n$ and refuses when $n = 2^D$
\src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:202-210}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Membership path and fold]
\label{def:ss-membership}
A path is a sibling list $(\sigma_0,\dots,\sigma_{D-1})$, leaf-to-root, and an
index $i$ \src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:99-105}. Natively the path is
rejected if $|\sigma| \ne D$ or $i \ge 2^D$; otherwise, with $b_h$ the bit $h$
of $i$,
\begin{equation}
  c_0 = \ell, \qquad
  c_{h+1} =
  \begin{cases}
    \Cp(\sigma_h, c_h) & b_h = 1,\\
    \Cp(c_h, \sigma_h) & b_h = 0,
  \end{cases}
  \qquad \mathsf{root} = c_D .
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/tree.rs:127-150}
\end{definition}

In-circuit, the input leaf $\mathsf{cm}$ is folded by the same recurrence
(\code{fold\_membership\_p2}): each $b_h$ is a private input constrained
boolean, each $\sigma_h$ enters as a circuit constant, and the side is chosen
with \code{select} \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:716-757}. The final digest
is constrained equal, by arithmetic difference, to the constant
$\mathsf{root}$ taken from the public statement
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:405-410,442-447}. The in-circuit fold iterates
over the supplied sibling list without checking $|\sigma| = D$
(\finding{L-05}), and the siblings being circuit constants is the subject of
\finding{H-03}.

\begin{definition}[Frontier]
\label{def:ss-frontier}
The append witness is a frontier $(f_0,\dots,f_{D-1};\ b_0,\dots,b_{D-1})$ with
$b_h \in \{0,1\}$ and implied count $n = \sum_h b_h 2^h$
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:144-174}. For $b_h = 1$, $f_h$ is
the root of the complete $2^h$-leaf block ending at the last leaf; slots with
$b_h = 0$ are unused \src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:144-148}. Its
root is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:ss-frontier-fold}
  c \leftarrow E_0;\quad \text{for } h = 0,\dots,D-1:\ \
  c \leftarrow
  \begin{cases}
    \Cp(f_h, c) & b_h = 1,\\
    \Cp(c, E_h) & b_h = 0,
  \end{cases}
  \qquad \mathsf{Fold}(f,b) = c .
\end{equation}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:191-204}
Native append of a leaf $\lambda$ carries upward: $g \leftarrow \lambda$; for
$h = 0,1,\dots$: if $b_h = 1$ then $g \leftarrow \Cp(f_h, g)$, $b_h \leftarrow 0$;
else $f_h \leftarrow g$, $b_h \leftarrow 1$ and stop; failing to stop means the
tree is full \src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:214-237}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[In-circuit append relation]
\label{def:ss-append}
\code{constrain\_append} takes $(f,b)$ as private inputs ($2$ extension elements
per slot, then $D$ bits, each constrained boolean)
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:489-506} and enforces, for a leaf
$\lambda$ and a pinned $R_{\mathrm{before}}$:
\begin{align}
  &\mathsf{Fold}(f,b) = R_{\mathrm{before}}
    && \text{\src{commitment\_gadget.rs:508-517}}\\
  &g_{-1} = \lambda,\quad g_h = b_h\ ?\ \Cp(f_h, g_{h-1}) : g_{h-1}
    && \text{\src{commitment\_gadget.rs:519-530}}\\
  &\kappa_0 = 1,\quad b'_h = b_h + \kappa_h - 2 b_h\kappa_h,\quad
    f'_h = \kappa_h(1-b_h)\ ?\ g_h : f_h,\quad \kappa_{h+1} = \kappa_h b_h
    && \text{\src{commitment\_gadget.rs:532-552}}\\
  &\kappa_D = 0
    && \text{\src{commitment\_gadget.rs:553-554}}\\
  &R_{\mathrm{after}} = \mathsf{Fold}(f', b')
    && \text{\src{commitment\_gadget.rs:556-557}}
\end{align}
The empty-subtree constants are derived at circuit-build time from
\code{Poseidon2Commitment} and enter as constants
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:117-133,577-585}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:557}.
\end{definition}

Within one transfer the appends chain: the first $R_{\mathrm{before}}$ is the
public $\mathsf{root}$ as a constant, output $i$'s $R_{\mathrm{after}}$ is output
$i{+}1$'s $R_{\mathrm{before}}$, and the last $R_{\mathrm{after}}$ is exported
and pinned to the public \code{root\_after}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:548-598,494-504}. The frontier witness for
output $i$ is computed natively by replaying the first $i$ outputs
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:610-626}. $R_{\mathrm{before}}$ constrains
$(f,b)$ only through Equation~\eqref{eq:ss-frontier-fold}; the root does not
separately commit to the count $n$, and $E_0$ coincides with the all-zero
digest, so $b$ is not uniquely determined by $R_{\mathrm{before}}$. The
resulting freedom in the append position is \finding{H-02}.

\begin{property}[On-chain treatment]
The settlement contract never folds the commitment tree. It stores
\code{currentRoot}, requires the block's \code{rootBefore} to equal it, and then
stores the proof-attested \code{rootAfter}
\src{contracts/src/ShieldedPool.sol:9-23,73-74,152-154,165}. The genesis root is
a constructor argument \src{contracts/src/ShieldedPool.sol:108-113,133}.
\end{property}

\subsection{Nullifier sparse Merkle tree}
\label{subsec:ss-smt}

\begin{definition}[Shape, address, leaf, empty constants]
\label{def:ss-smt}
The nullifier map is a sparse binary Merkle tree of depth
$D_{\mathsf{nf}} = \code{NULLIFIER\_TREE\_DEPTH} = 96$
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:64}. For a nullifier digest
$\mathsf{nf} \in \{0,1\}^{256}$ with bytes $\mathsf{nf}[0..32)$, the address is
\begin{equation}
  a(\mathsf{nf}) = (a_0,\dots,a_{95}), \qquad
  a_i = \big\lfloor \mathsf{nf}[\lfloor i/8 \rfloor] / 2^{\,i \bmod 8} \big\rfloor \bmod 2 ,
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the low $96$ bits of the byte string, little-endian within each byte
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:66-70}. The leaf value stored at
$a(\mathsf{nf})$ is the full digest $\mathsf{nf}$
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:200-204,307-318}. The empty
constants follow the same recurrence and hasher as the commitment tree,
$E_0 = 0^{256}$, $E_h = \Cp(E_{h-1},E_{h-1})$ for $1 \le h \le 96$
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:128-140}, so the two trees share
$E_0,\dots,E_{32}$, and the empty-map root is $E_{96}$
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:187-189}.
\end{definition}

Since bytes $0..11$ of a digest are $\mathrm{LE32}(d_0)\|\mathrm{LE32}(d_1)\|\mathrm{LE32}(d_2)$,
the address bits are $a_{32k+j} = $ bit $j$ of $d_k$ for $k \in \{0,1,2\}$,
$0 \le j < 32$. Every digest emitted by $\Hp$ has canonical elements
$d_k < p < 2^{31}$ \src{crates/pq-hash/src/poseidon.rs:155-163}, so
$a_{31} = a_{63} = a_{95} = 0$ for every honestly derived nullifier.

\begin{definition}[Native root]
For the spent set $S$ (32-byte strings) and $A \subseteq S$,
\begin{equation}
  T(h, A) \;=\;
  \begin{cases}
    E_h & A = \emptyset,\\
    \text{first element of } A & h = 0,\\
    \Cp\big(T(h-1, A_0),\, T(h-1, A_1)\big), \ \ A_\beta = \{x \in A : a_{h-1}(x) = \beta\} & \text{otherwise},
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:196-220}
and $\mathsf{root}_{\mathsf{nf}} = T(96, S)$
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:180-183}.
\end{definition}

\begin{specdelta}[Address coincidences in the native map]
\label{delta:ss-smt-collide}
The depth comment states that two nullifiers agreeing on the $96$ address bits
``are treated as duplicates (the insert refuses)''
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:57-63}. The enforced behaviour
differs: the spent set is a \code{HashSet} keyed on all $32$ bytes
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:122,172-176}, so \code{insert} accepts
a second nullifier with an equal address; at height $0$, $T$ returns the first
element of $A$ in the set's iteration order
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:181,200-204}; and
\code{highest\_differing\_bit} returns \code{None} for such a pair, so the
witness builder does not bucket it
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:78-92,265-272}. See
\finding{M-09}. The module header still describes full 256-bit addressing
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:13-22,45-49}; see \finding{I-02}.
\end{specdelta}

\begin{definition}[Fold and native witness]
For a start height $s$, start node $x$, siblings $(\sigma_s,\dots,\sigma_{95})$
and address $a$,
\begin{equation}
  \mathsf{Fold}_s(x;\sigma;a):\quad c_s = x,\qquad
  c_{h+1} =
  \begin{cases}
    \Cp(\sigma_h, c_h) & a_h = 1,\\
    \Cp(c_h, \sigma_h) & a_h = 0,
  \end{cases}
  \qquad \text{output } c_{96}.
\end{equation}
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:223-241}
For an unspent $\mathsf{nf}$, the witness has start height
$h^\ast = \min_{x \in S} \mathrm{hdb}(a(\mathsf{nf}), a(x))$ (or $96$ if no
spent address differs), where $\mathrm{hdb}$ is the highest differing address
bit, and siblings $\sigma_h = T(h, B_h)$ for $h^\ast \le h < 96$, with $B_h$ the
spent addresses whose highest differing bit is $h$
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:250-283}. Natively,
\begin{align}
  R_{\mathrm{before}} &= \mathsf{Fold}_{h^\ast}\big(E_{h^\ast};\ \sigma;\ a(\mathsf{nf})\big)
    && \text{\src{nullifier\_tree.rs:292-299}}\\
  R_{\mathrm{after}} &= \mathsf{Fold}_{0}\big(\mathsf{nf};\ (E_0,\dots,E_{h^\ast-1},\sigma_{h^\ast},\dots,\sigma_{95});\ a(\mathsf{nf})\big)
    && \text{\src{nullifier\_tree.rs:307-318}}
\end{align}
The standalone checker additionally requires $h^\ast \le 96$ and
$|\sigma| = 96 - h^\ast$ and compares the folded root bytewise
\src{crates/shielded/src/nullifier\_tree.rs:328-359}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[In-circuit absence and insertion]
\label{def:ss-smt-circuit}
The circuit fixes $\code{FOLD\_DEPTH} = 32$ and
$\code{FOLD\_START} = 96 - 32 = 64$
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:71-74}. The native witness is padded
to heights $64..95$ by inserting $E_h$ for $64 \le h < h^\ast$, and proving is
refused when $h^\ast < 64$
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:128-155}. With $\mathsf{nf}$ the
in-circuit nullifier digest, the address bits are the 16-bit decompositions of
its first $6$ exported limbs $L(\mathsf{nf})_0,\dots,L(\mathsf{nf})_5$
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:76-78,166-182}, and the gadget
enforces
\begin{align}
  R_{\mathrm{before}} &= \mathsf{Fold}_{64}\big(E_{64};\ (\sigma_{64},\dots,\sigma_{95});\ a(\mathsf{nf})\big)
    && \text{\src{nullifier\_gadget.rs:271-276}}\\
  R_{\mathrm{after}} &= \mathsf{Fold}_{0}\big(\mathsf{nf};\ (E_0,\dots,E_{63},\sigma_{64},\dots,\sigma_{95});\ a(\mathsf{nf})\big)
    && \text{\src{nullifier\_gadget.rs:285-288}}
\end{align}
where every $\sigma_h$ and $E_h$ enters as a circuit constant built from the
prover-side witness (\code{const\_digest})
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:191-197,203-231}. The 31-bit
decomposition behind $L(\cdot)$ is constrained canonical
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:417-431}
\src{vendor/p3-recursion/circuit/src/builder/circuit\_builder.rs:1435-1449}, and
odd-indexed limbs are $15$-bit high halves, so $a(\mathsf{nf})$ is uniquely
determined by the digest and $a_{31} = a_{63} = a_{95} = 0$ in-circuit as well.
\end{definition}

Across the spends of one transfer the root is threaded: the first
$R_{\mathrm{before}}$ is the public \code{nullifier\_roots.before} as a constant,
each spend's $R_{\mathrm{before}}$ is constrained equal to the running root, the
running root becomes that spend's $R_{\mathrm{after}}$, and the final running
root is constrained equal to the public \code{nullifier\_roots.after}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:390-398,456-470,513-525}.

\begin{property}[What in-circuit absence certifies]
\label{prop:ss-absence}
Because the absence fold starts from the constant $E_{64}$ at height $64$, a
satisfying assignment asserts (under collision resistance of $\Cp$) that the
height-$64$ node on the path selected by $a_{64},\dots,a_{95}$ is empty under
$R_{\mathrm{before}}$, i.e.\ that no leaf has an address agreeing with
$a(\mathsf{nf})$ on bits $64$ through $95$; the low bits $a_0,\dots,a_{63}$
enter only the insertion fold, which places $\mathsf{nf}$ as the sole leaf of
that height-$64$ subtree. Bits $a_{64..95}$ are the bits of the third digest
element $d_2$, so the in-circuit absence predicate separates nullifiers by
$d_2$ alone. The spendability consequences of this, together with
Spec delta~\ref{delta:ss-smt-collide}, are \finding{M-09}.
\end{property}

\begin{property}[On-chain treatment]
The contract holds no nullifier set. It stores \code{currentNullifierRoot},
requires the block's \code{nullifierBefore} to equal it, and stores the
attested \code{nullifierAfter}
\src{contracts/src/ShieldedPool.sol:76-77,155-157,166}. A zero genesis argument
selects the pinned constant
\src{contracts/src/ShieldedPool.sol:79-86,133-135}
\begin{Verbatim}[fontsize=\small]
EMPTY_NULLIFIER_ROOT =
  0x83265b7a2c40cc6eca237b6016b764606335cd0c6ff0732470e20a484d5a7329
\end{Verbatim}
documented as the Poseidon2 $E_{96}$; the golden-vector generator derives
\code{nullifier\_before\_hex} from an empty
\code{NullifierMap<Poseidon2Commitment>} and the vector file carries the same
bytes \src{crates/prover/tests/golden\_vectors.rs:413,495}
\src{contracts/test/vectors/block\_vectors.json:4}. The constructor comment
still describes a depth-256 Keccak empty root
\src{contracts/src/ShieldedPool.sol:114-117} (\finding{I-02}).
\end{property}
