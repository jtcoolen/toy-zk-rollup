\section{The transfer relation}
\label{sec:transfer}

This section states the relation $R_{\mathsf{transfer}}$ that the client
transfer circuit enforces for one shielded transfer with $k$ inputs (spends) and
$m$ outputs. The whole relation is laid into a builder by
\code{constrain\_transfer} \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:358-537}; the
stand-alone client circuit wraps it with the Poseidon2 permutation and
recompose tables enabled, exports the returned expressions as the statement,
and witnesses it \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:645-701}. The block circuit
reuses the same function unchanged inside a larger builder
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:328-334}. Everything below is read from the
constraints the code emits; the module-level description of the relation
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:1-62} is used only where the code agrees
with it. Hash and encoding notation ($\Hp$, $\Cp$, $\limbs$, $L$) is that of
\S\ref{sec:primitives}.

\subsection{In-circuit gadgets}

All circuit values are elements of $\K$ (the builder is
\code{CircuitBuilder<Challenge>}). A digest is carried as a pair in $\K^2$.

\begin{definition}[Digest constants]
For a 32-byte digest $d$, $\langle d\rangle\in\K^2$ is \code{digest\_to\_ext}$(d)$:
the strict decoder of \S\ref{sec:primitives} followed by packing base
coefficients $0..3$ and $4..7$ into one $\K$ element each; a non-canonical word
yields no value and the circuit is not built.
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:101-106}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compression gadget $\Cp^{\mathrm{c}}$]
$\Cp^{\mathrm{c}}(l,r)$ for $l,r\in\K^2$ is one row of the width-16 Poseidon2
table with \code{new\_start} set, all four extension slots supplied as
$(l_0,l_1,r_0,r_1)$, and the two rate outputs returned.
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:265-287}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Sponge gadget $\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}$]
$\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}(x)$ for a limb vector $x$ is \code{p2\_sponge\_limbs}: $x$ is cut into
chunks of $8$ base elements; each chunk is recomposed into the two rate slots
and absorbed by one permutation row. The first row has \code{new\_start} set and
supplies only the two rate slots; later rows chain the capacity through the
table. In a short final chunk a partially filled rate slot is completed with
base coefficients decomposed from the previous row's output, and a wholly
empty rate slot is left unsupplied. The output is the last row's two rate
elements; $\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}(\varepsilon)=(0,0)$.
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:306-367}
The framed form $\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}(D;\,p_1,\dots)$ is $\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}(\limbs(D)\,\|\,p_1\,\|\cdots)$
(\code{p2\_framed}).
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:254-264}
\end{definition}

The gadget is intended to mirror the native overwrite sponge of
Def.~\ref{def:sponge}. In the Poseidon2 AIR the chain-start zero-capacity
constraint applies only to challenger sponge rows (a challenger-only table, or
the trusted challenger-start selector of a shared table); on a table that also
carries caller-supplied rows a \code{new\_start} row's capacity is whatever the
caller fed it
\src{vendor/p3-recursion/poseidon2-circuit-air/src/air.rs:119-128,1166-1185}.
The configuration these rows are keyed by has \code{challenger: false}
\src{vendor/p3-recursion/circuit/src/ops/poseidon2\_perm/config.rs:135-145}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:69}. Consequently
$\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}$ does not bind its input as the native $\Hp$ does (initial
capacity and short-final-chunk handling) \finding{V-07}. The preimage lengths
used by this relation are:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X c c c@{}}
\toprule
Use & Limbs & Perm.\ rows & Short final chunk \\
\midrule
$\mathsf{pk}$ derivation (T2): $\limbs(D_{\mathsf{PK}})\|\mathit{sk}$ & $10+16=26$ & 4 & yes (2 limbs) \\
leaf / commitment (T3, T6): $\limbs(D_{\mathsf{NOTE}})\|v\|\rho\|\psi\|\mathit{pk}$ & $64$ & 8 & no \\
nullifier (T5): $\limbs(D_{\mathsf{NF}})\|\mathit{sk}\|\rho$ & $11+16+16=43$ & 6 & yes (3 limbs) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
(The leaf preimage is $12+4+16+16+16=64$ limbs.)

\begin{definition}[Digest export $L$]
$L(y)$ for $y\in\K^2$ is \code{export\_digest\_limbs}: each $\K$ element is
decomposed into its 4 base coefficients by ALU rows, each coefficient into 31
bits, and each bit vector recombined into a low 16-bit and a high 15-bit limb,
giving 16 limbs. \src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:417-431}
\end{definition}

Selection is $\mathsf{sel}(c,x,y)=x$ if $c=1$ and $y$ if $c=0$, with $c$
boolean-constrained at the call site
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:742-753}.

\begin{definition}[Path fold]
For $x\in\K^2$, constants $s_0,\dots,s_{n-1}\in\K^2$ and boolean expressions
$a_0,\dots,a_{n-1}$,
\begin{align}
  c_0 &= x, \qquad
  c_{\ell+1} = \Cp^{\mathrm{c}}\big(\mathsf{sel}(a_\ell,s_\ell,c_\ell),\ \mathsf{sel}(a_\ell,c_\ell,s_\ell)\big),\\
  \mathsf{Fold}(x,s,a) &= c_n ,
\end{align}
i.e.\ $a_\ell=1$ places the sibling on the left. The membership fold
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:716-757} and the nullifier-map fold
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:203-231} are both of this form.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Frontier fold and append]
Let $E_h=\langle\mathrm{empty}_h\rangle$ be the commitment-tree empty-subtree
constants for $h<32$ \src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:121-133}.
For $f\in(\K^2)^{32}$ and booleans $b\in\{0,1\}^{32}$,
\begin{align}
  y_0 &= (0,0), &
  y_{h+1} &= \Cp^{\mathrm{c}}\big(\mathsf{sel}(b_h,f_h,y_h),\ \mathsf{sel}(b_h,y_h,E_h)\big), &
  \mathsf{FoldF}(f,b) &= y_{32},
\end{align}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:570-599}, the circuit form of the
native \code{fold\_to\_root} \src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:191-204}.
For a leaf $C\in\K^2$, $\mathsf{Append}(f,b,C)$ is defined by
\begin{align}
  g_{-1} &= C, & g_h &= \mathsf{sel}\big(b_h,\ \Cp^{\mathrm{c}}(f_h,g_{h-1}),\ g_{h-1}\big),\\
  \kappa_0 &= 1, & \kappa_{h+1} &= \kappa_h b_h,\\
  b'_h &= b_h+\kappa_h-2\kappa_h b_h, & f'_h &= \mathsf{sel}\big(\kappa_h-\kappa_h b_h,\ g_h,\ f_h\big),
\end{align}
with the constraint $\kappa_{32}=0$ (the tree is not full), and
$\mathsf{Append}(f,b,C)=\mathsf{FoldF}(f',b')$.
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:519-557}
\end{definition}

\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:727-734}; each output's recipient key

\end{itemize}

\subsection{The relation}

\begin{definition}[$R_{\mathsf{transfer}}$]
\label{def:rtransfer}
$(x;\,w)\in R_{\mathsf{transfer}}$ for the circuit built from constants as above
iff all of the following hold.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T1 (ranges).} Every limb of $\mathit{sk}_i,\rho_i,\psi_i,\rho'_j,\psi'_j$
lies in $[0,2^{16})$ (16-bit decomposition) \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:191-207};
for every amount $w\in\{v_i\}\cup\{v'_j\}\cup\{\varphi\}$, $w_0,w_1,w_2\in[0,2^{16})$
and $w_3\in[0,2^{14})$, so $\mathrm{val}(w)\in[0,2^{62})$
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:157-172}; $d_1,d_2,d_3\in[0,2^{8})$
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:827-829}; every $a_{i,\ell}$ and every
entry of $b_j$ is boolean \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:742}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:501-506}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T2 (ownership).} For each input,
\[
  P_i = \Hp^{\mathrm{c}}\big(D_{\mathsf{PK}};\ \mathit{sk}_i\big)\in\K^2 .
\]
$P_i$ is computed, never supplied; it constrains the witness only through T3.
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:423-426}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T3 (input leaf).}
\[
  \lambda_i = \Hp^{\mathrm{c}}\big(\limbs(D_{\mathsf{NOTE}})\,\|\,v_i\,\|\,\rho_i\,\|\,\psi_i\,\|\,L(P_i)\big).
\]
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:428-435}
This is the circuit form of the native
$\code{commit}=\Hp(D_{\mathsf{NOTE}},\mathrm{LE64}(v),\rho,\psi,\mathit{pk})$, since
$\limbs(\mathrm{LE64}(v))=v$ by \code{split\_value}
\src{crates/shielded/src/note.rs:108-111}\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:224-231}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T4 (membership).} With $n_i=|s_i|$,
\[
  \mathsf{Fold}\big(\lambda_i,\ s_i,\ (a_{i,0},\dots,a_{i,n_i-1})\big) = \langle rt\rangle ,
\]
as two equalities in $\K$. \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:437-447}
The index bits are tied to nothing else; $n_i$ is the length of the supplied
path and is not compared to \code{DEPTH} \finding{L-05}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T5 (nullifier and map transition).} For each input,
\[
  N_i = \Hp^{\mathrm{c}}\big(D_{\mathsf{NF}};\ \mathit{sk}_i,\ \rho_i\big),
\]
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:456}
and the address bits are $u_{i,16q+r} = $ bit $r$ of $L(N_i)_q$ for $q<6$,
$r<16$ (so bits $31,63,95$ are identically zero, the high limbs being 15-bit)
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:166-182,262-263}. With
$R_0=\langle R_{\mathsf{before}}\rangle$,
\begin{align}
  \mathsf{Fold}\big(\epsilon_{i,64},\ \sigma_i,\ (u_{i,64},\dots,u_{i,95})\big) &= R_{i-1}
  &&\text{(absence)}\\
  R_i &= \mathsf{Fold}\big(N_i,\ (\epsilon_{i,0},\dots,\epsilon_{i,63})\,\|\,\sigma_i,\ (u_{i,0},\dots,u_{i,95})\big)
  &&\text{(insertion)}
\end{align}
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:265-290}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:457-470}
and finally $R_k=\langle R_{\mathsf{after}}\rangle$
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:513-525}. For $k=0$ this forces
$R_{\mathsf{before}}=R_{\mathsf{after}}$.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T6 (outputs and commitment-tree transition).} For each output,
\[
  C_j = \Hp^{\mathrm{c}}\big(\limbs(D_{\mathsf{NOTE}})\,\|\,v'_j\,\|\,\rho'_j\,\|\,\psi'_j\,\|\,\limbs(\mathit{pk}'_j)\big),
\]
where $\limbs(\mathit{pk}'_j)$ is a circuit constant
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:573-581}. With $T_0=\langle rt\rangle$,
\begin{align}
  \mathsf{FoldF}(f_j,b_j) &= T_{j-1}, &
  T_j &= \mathsf{Append}(f_j,b_j,C_j), &
  L(T_m) &= \limbs(rt') .
\end{align}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:557-598}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:508-517}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:498-503}
Each append allocates its own $(f_j,b_j)$, constrained only through
$\mathsf{FoldF}(f_j,b_j)=T_{j-1}$ \finding{H-02}. For $m=0$, $T_0=\langle rt\rangle$
and the pin forces $rt'=rt$.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T7 (value conservation).} With
$\beta=\max(k,m+1)$, $A_t=\sum_{i}v_{i,t}$, $B_t=\varphi_t+\sum_{j}v'_{j,t}$,
$d_0=d_4=\beta$ (constants), for $t=0,1,2,3$:
\begin{equation}
  A_t + 2^{16}\,d_{t+1} \;=\; B_t + d_t + \beta\,(2^{16}-1)
  \qquad\text{in }\K .
\end{equation}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:792-844}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{T8 (shape).} $\max(k,m+1)\le 16$; the number of
nullifier witnesses equals $k$; $|\sigma_i|=32$, $|\epsilon_i|=65$; $|f_j|=|b_j|=32$.
These are build-time checks that refuse to construct the circuit, not
constraints. \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:366-383}
\src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:249-260}
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:482-487}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Emission order.} Constraints are laid in the order: for each input
$i=1..k$ in spend order, T1 (secrets, amount), T2, T3, T4, T5 (nullifier,
absence against $R_{i-1}$, insertion, export of $L(N_i)$); then for each output
$j=1..m$ in output order, T1, T6 (commitment, export of $L(C_j)$, append);
then the root exports, the $R_k$ pin, the fee range check, and T7.
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:413-534}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:565-597}
Because $R_{i-1}$ is the previous spend's insertion output, the order of
nullifiers within a transfer is part of the relation.
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:385-389}

\subsection{Value conservation over the integers}

The column constraint as emitted:
\begin{Verbatim}[fontsize=\small]
// A_j + 2^16 d_{j+1} - B_j - d_j - bias(2^16 - 1) = 0
let lhs = builder.mul_add(two_16, next_expr, a_expr);
let carries = builder.add(b_expr, carry_expr);
let rhs = builder.add(carries, offset_expr);
let diff = builder.sub(lhs, rhs);
builder.assert_zero(diff);
\end{Verbatim}
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:833-838}

\begin{property}[T7 is an integer identity]
\label{prop:balance}
If T1, T7 and T8 hold then
$\sum_{i=1}^{k}\mathrm{val}(v_i) = \sum_{j=1}^{m}\mathrm{val}(v'_j)+\mathrm{val}(\varphi)$ in $\mathbb{Z}$.
\end{property}

\noindent\emph{Derivation.} By T1 every term is a small non-negative integer.
With $k,m+1,\beta\le 16$ (T8), each side of a column equation is bounded by
\[
  A_t + 2^{16}d_{t+1} \le 16(2^{16}-1) + 2^{16}(2^{8}-1) < 2^{25},\qquad
  B_t + d_t + \beta(2^{16}-1) \le 32(2^{16}-1) + 2^{8} < 2^{22},
\]
(the top column has $d_4=\beta\le16$), both below $p>2^{30}$, so each field
equation is an integer equation \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:127-135}.
Multiplying column $t$ by $2^{16t}$ and summing telescopes the carries:
\[
  \sum_t 2^{16t}(A_t-B_t) = d_0 - 2^{64}d_4 + \beta(2^{64}-1) = 0
  \quad\text{since } d_0=d_4=\beta ,
\]
and $\sum_t 2^{16t}A_t=\sum_i\mathrm{val}(v_i)$,
$\sum_t 2^{16t}B_t=\mathrm{val}(\varphi)+\sum_j\mathrm{val}(v'_j)$.
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:759-791}
The honest prover computes $d_{t+1}=\lfloor(B_t+d_t+\beta(2^{16}-1)-A_t)/2^{16}\rfloor$
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:826}. \hfill$\square$

\subsection{Scope of the relation}

The following are stated neutrally as properties of what is and is not
enforced; impact is discussed in the findings report.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Sponge binding.} T2, T3, T5 and T6 rest on $\Hp^{\mathrm{c}}$,
  whose first-row capacity and short-final-chunk completion are not pinned as
  in the native definition; the nullifier and $\mathsf{pk}$ preimages have short
  final chunks \finding{V-07}.
\item \textbf{Instance-specific constants.} Membership siblings $s_i$ and
  recipient keys $\limbs(\mathit{pk}'_j)$ are circuit constants, hence part of
  the preprocessed circuit description rather than of the witness
  \finding{H-03}. The nullifier-map siblings $\sigma_i$ and lower empties
  $\epsilon_i$ are likewise constants and are checked by count only.
\item \textbf{Append position.} The frontier $f_j$ and count bits $b_j$ are fresh
  private inputs at every append, related to the tree only through
  $\mathsf{FoldF}(f_j,b_j)=T_{j-1}$; the count $\sum_h 2^h b_{j,h}$ is not
  carried from one append to the next \finding{H-02}.
\item \textbf{Path length.} $n_i$ is not constrained to equal \code{DEPTH}
  \finding{L-05}.
\item \textbf{Spend authorization.} No SPHINCS+ verification and no
  \code{DOMAIN\_TX} message appear in \code{constrain\_transfer}; spend
  authority inside $R_{\mathsf{transfer}}$ is knowledge of $\mathit{sk}_i$
  satisfying T2--T5 \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:358-537}
  \src{crates/shielded/src/signing.rs:35} \finding{M-02}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Spec deltas}

\begin{specdelta}[Nullifier map is Poseidon2, not Keccak]
Two comments still describe the nullifier map as Keccak:
\src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:15-16} and the proving entry point's
list of non-primitive operations \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:904-906}.
The enforced map fold is $\Cp^{\mathrm{c}}$ on the same Poseidon2 table as the
note tree \src{crates/prover/src/nullifier\_gadget.rs:203-231}, and only the
Poseidon2 and recompose tables are enabled for the client circuit
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:657-661}.
\end{specdelta}

\begin{specdelta}[Digest export is canonical]
The export comment states that each 31-bit coefficient decomposition admits one
non-canonical witness \src{crates/prover/src/commitment\_gadget.rs:408-412}.
The vendored \code{decompose\_to\_bits} additionally pins every full-width
($31$-bit for $\F$) chunk below $p$
\src{vendor/p3-recursion/circuit/src/builder/circuit\_builder.rs:1435-1449}, so
$L(y)$ is the unique canonical limb vector of $y$.
\end{specdelta}

\begin{specdelta}[One frontier per append, not one per transfer]
The entry-point documentation describes a single frontier witness ``at
\code{public.root}'' that the gadget pins before appending
\src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:346-351}. The circuit instead takes a
separately allocated $(f_j,b_j)$ per output, derived natively by
\code{frontier\_step} \src{crates/prover/src/transfer.rs:600-626}, and pins
each only against the running root $T_{j-1}$ (T6) \finding{H-02}.
\end{specdelta}
